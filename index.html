Eligibility & Requirements

  • Qualification:

    Minimum 60% aggregate in 10+2 (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics).

  • Entrance Exams:

    Valid score in JEE Main, State CET, or Apex Tech Entrance Test (ATET).

Apply for Admission

Admission Process

Step 1: Application

Fill the online application form and submit required documents.

Step 2: Document Verification

Our admission team reviews your academic transcripts online.

Step 3: Entrance Score Review

Shortlisting based on merit and entrance examination scores.

Step 4: Confirmation

Issuance of provisional admission letter to selected candidates.

Step 5: Enrollment

Fee payment and final campus reporting for orientation.
